## Summary

The video presents a scalping strategy built around a single concept: the **4-hour range** — the high and low of the *first 4-hour candle of the day*, measured in **New York time**. The strategy requires no indicators and follows a three-step checklist.

### The 3-Step Checklist

**Step 1 — Mark the 4-hour range**
- Set the chart timeframe to 4 hours and the chart timezone to **New York time** (important — the range must be based on NY time).
- Find the first 4-hour candle that formed on the current date.
- Draw a line at its high and low, extending both to the end of the day.
- Wait until the candle has **fully closed** before marking the range.

**Step 2 — The scalping setup (5-minute chart)**
- Drop to the 5-minute chart and wait for a 5-minute candle to **close outside** the range (wicks alone don't count).
- Then wait for price to **re-enter and close back inside** the range.
- Both the breakout and the re-entry must happen **within the same trading day** as the marked range.

**Step 3 — Entry, stop loss, take profit**
- **Direction:** Break above the range high then re-enter → short. Break below the range low then re-enter → long (i.e., trade the failed breakout / trap direction).
- **Stop loss:** At the exact extreme of the breakout move (range high/low). If the breakout is very large, use the nearest key level (resistance/order block) instead of the exact extreme.
- **Take profit:** 2× the stop-loss distance (2R target).

### Trade Logic
The strategy is a **stop-hunt / false-breakout reversal** play: price sweeping outside the daily 4-hour range and failing to hold is treated as a trap, and the trade is taken back into the range. Multiple valid setups can be taken per day; if price leaves the range and never re-enters, no more trades for that day.
